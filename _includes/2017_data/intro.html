Welcome to the Canadian Design Workshop

The Canadian Design Workshop (CDW) is a biennial gathering of design educators and researchers from across Canada to advance engineering design education in Canada.

After three successful prior events, the Canadian Design Workshop will be back in 2026. Participants at CDW will share ongoing design education research from institutions across Canada, establish a collective research agenda, sustain continued and increased collaboration to grow Canadian capacity in this area.

More details about the 2026 Workshop will be announced soon.
